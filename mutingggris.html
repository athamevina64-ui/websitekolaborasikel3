How an Old Keyboard Is Transformed into a Smart Trash Bin (Tong Sampah Pintar)

Paragraph 1 - General Statement

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. Old computer keyboards are often discarded when they are damaged or no longer needed. However, these keyboards contain materials that can still be reused. One creative way to manage this waste is by transforming old keyboard keys into a smart trash bin. This transformation shows how electronic waste can become a useful, valuable, and technological product.

Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process

The transformation begins when an unused keyboard is separated into individual components. Its plastic keys are removed from the main keyboard structure and separated from other electronic parts. The keys are then cleaned to remove dust and dirt. At the same time, a used trash bin body is prepared and an ultrasonic sensor (HC-SR04) is prepared as the automatic system. At this stage, the original function of the keyboard keys changes. Instead of being used for typing, they become decorative materials for a new smart product.

Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product

The cleaned keys are combined with a plastic trash bin frame to create a new object. Through the process of arranging and attaching, the individual keys form a unique decorative layer around the bin. An ultrasonic sensor is installed on the front part and connected to a microcontroller and servo motor to make the lid open automatically. Different letters, numbers, and symbols create a colorful technological appearance. Once the keys are firmly attached, the sensor is installed, and the wiring is connected, the separate components become one complete smart decorative product.

Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact

As a result, an old computer keyboard is transformed into a smart trash bin that has functional, aesthetic, and technological value. The product can be used to dispose of trash without touching it, because the lid opens automatically when a hand is detected, while also serving as a creative decoration. This transformation reduces the amount of electronic waste and encourages the reuse of available materials. Therefore, recycling computer components into smart products is one way to support a more sustainable and hygienic environment.
