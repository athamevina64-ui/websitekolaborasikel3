How a Smart Trash Bin Is Transformed into a Smart and Clean Environment

Paragraph 1 – General Statement

Electronic waste (e-waste), such as old keyboards, is part of the ecocycle problem in today's digital world. Instead of throwing them away, we can reuse them as valuable materials. This project transforms a used keyboard into a smart trash bin, helping to reduce electronic waste and support a cleaner environment.

Paragraph 2 – The Transformation Process

The transformation begins by preparing the electronic components, such as Arduino Uno, an ultrasonic sensor, a servo motor, jumper wires, and a trash bin. The used keyboard is cleaned and its keys are removed, then attached to the front of the trash bin. The sensors and servo motor are installed and connected to the Arduino Uno, which is programmed to control the system.

Paragraph 3 – The Formation of a New Product

After all components are connected, the program is uploaded to the Arduino Uno. When someone brings their hand close to the sensor, the sensor detects the object and sends a signal to the Arduino. Then, the servo motor moves to open the trash bin lid for five seconds. After that, the lid closes automatically. The finished product is a smart trash bin that is easy to use in homes, schools, and public places.

Paragraph 4 – The Result and Its Impact

As a result, the smart trash bin has both functional and environmental value. The purpose of using a used keyboard is to reduce electronic waste, save costs, increase creativity, support environmental awareness, and encourage recycling. The benefits are:

  • Reducing e-waste and preventing pollution
  • Saving costs by reusing old keyboards
  • Encouraging creativity and innovation
  • Promoting a cleaner and greener environment
