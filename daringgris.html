How an Automatic Smart Trash Bin Works

1. General Statement

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the rapid development of technology. One example is an old keyboard that is no longer used. However, the old keyboard can still be reused as a material to create creative products. In this project, an old keyboard is used as one of the materials to make an automatic smart trash bin. This product combines the reuse of old materials with Arduino Uno, an ultrasonic sensor, and a servo motor to create a more practical and modern trash bin.

2. The Transformation Process

The process begins by preparing the electronic components, such as Arduino Uno, an ultrasonic sensor, a servo motor, jumper wires, and a trash bin. The components are connected according to the circuit design. The Arduino Uno is programmed to control the system, while the ultrasonic sensor detects objects around the trash bin. The servo motor is used to open and close the lid automatically.

How to Install the Keyboard:

To install the keyboard, first remove all the keys and clean the keyboard. Then, cut and shape the keyboard according to the size of the trash bin. Finally, arrange the pieces and glue them onto the front of the trash bin as a decorative panel, making sure the connections are neatly concealed.

3. The Formation of a New Product

After all components are connected, the program is uploaded to the Arduino Uno. When someone brings their hand close to the sensor, the sensor detects the object and sends a signal to the Arduino. Then, the servo motor moves to open the trash bin lid for five seconds. After that, the lid closes automatically. All components work together to create an automatic smart trash bin.

4. The Result and Its Impact

As a result, an automatic smart trash bin is created. This product makes it easier for people to throw away trash without touching the lid directly. It is more practical, hygienic, and can help keep the environment cleaner.

The Purpose and Benefits of Using Used Keyboards:

The use of old keyboards aims to reduce electronic waste by turning unused items into something more useful. In addition to saving costs, reusing old keyboards can also enhance creativity, increase awareness of environmental protection, and promote the importance of recycling.
